What it is

Meet Imarisha Dereva

A retirement savings plan created through a partnership between LAPFUND and Little Cab, built specifically for drivers on the platform.

Imarisha + Dereva
In Swahili, imarisha means “to strengthen,” and dereva means “driver.” Strengthening every driver’s financial future, one ride at a time.

LAPFUND (Local Authorities Provident Fund) is a state corporation that manages retirement funds under Kenyan law. Under this partnership, it opens and manages an individual account for every driver who joins.

Questions

Good to know

What is Imarisha Dereva?
A retirement savings plan created through a partnership between LAPFUND and Little Cab, built for drivers on the platform.
Who can join?
Drivers registered on the Little Cab platform.
How are contributions made?
Ksh 20 is set aside per eligible corporate trip, up to Ksh 100 a day from your first 5 trips, and channelled automatically into your LAPFUND account.
How much should I contribute?
Under the current structure, contributions total about Ksh 3,000 a month. You can always add more through voluntary top-ups via M-Ratiba.
Can I add extra savings?
Yes, anytime, through M-Ratiba on the M-PESA app.
How do I check my balance?
Through the Little Cab wallet or LAPFUND’s own platforms, including a mini-statement of recent transactions.
What is the minimum saving period?
Your savings mature after 3 years of continuous contributions. At maturity, you can claim your benefits or continue contributing for a further 3-year cycle.
What is Group Life Assurance, and when does it apply?
Added protection for you and your family. It activates once you contribute at least Ksh 2,000 a month (or Ksh 24,000 a year) and stay active and consistent for one year.
